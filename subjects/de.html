Barrel shifter & ALU
✎ Barrel Shifter aur ALU - ekdum simple (Instinct) - video nahi mila, khud padhao+

Barrel Shifter: aisa circuit jo data ke bits ko ek hi clock me kitne bhi positions left/right shift kar de.

Jaise musical chairs - sab log ek saath apni seat se uth ke 2 seat aage baith gaye. Ek round me hi poora shift. Normal shift register har tick pe sirf 1 seat shift karta hai; barrel shifter ek tick me 2, 3, 4... jo bolo.

Kaise banta hai: MUX (multiplexers) ki lines se. Har output bit ke liye ek MUX jo choose karta hai ki kaunsa input bit wahan aayega. Shift amount (kitna shift karna hai) MUX ke select lines pe jaata hai.

ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit): processor ka wo hissa jo calculation (add, subtract) aur logic (AND, OR, XOR) dono karta hai.

  • Select lines decide karti hain kaunsa operation hoga: 00 = ADD, 01 = SUB, 10 = AND, 11 = OR (example).
  • Andar: full adders + logic gates + ek MUX jo final answer chunta hai.

Exam tip: "ALU = adder + logic gates + MUX" - ye 3 cheezein likh ke diagram banao, marks pakka.

Pulse train generator & PRBS generator
✎ Pulse Train aur PRBS Generator - ekdum simple (Instinct) - video nahi mila, khud padhao+

Pulse Train Generator = aisa circuit jo har clock tick pe ek fixed pattern deta hai, baar-baar. Jaise: 1, 0, 1, 1, 0 ... phir wapas 1, 0, 1, 1, 0 ... repeat.

Socho ek line me log khade hain. Har "tick" pe sab ek kadam aage badhte hain, aur jo aadmi line ke end pe hai woh bahar nikal jata hai. Jo pattern bahar aata hai, wahi pulse train hai.

Kaise banate hain: Shift register (flip-flops ki line) + ek feedback wire jo decide karti hai ki line ke start me kya wapas daalna hai.

Chhota example: Pattern 101 chahiye (length 3). 3 flip-flops lo: Q1 Q2 Q3. Har tick pe sab aage shift hote hain, feedback se start me naya bit aata hai. Pattern baar-baar aata rahega: 101, 101, 101...

Clock generation: Ek T flip-flop input frequency ko half kar deta hai. Aise crystal oscillator se slow, steady clock banti hai.

PRBS (Pseudo-Random Binary Sequence): Ek magic box jo "random" jaisi numbers deta hai - par asal me fixed rule se, isliye pseudo (naqli) random.

Kaise banta hai: Shift register me beech ke kuch outputs ka XOR karke feedback daalte hain. Ise LFSR kehte hain. n flip-flops ho to 2^n - 1 alag states aati hain, phir repeat. (All-zero state allowed nahi, warna circuit wahi atak jayega.)

Chhota example: 3 flip-flops, feedback = Q3 XOR Q1, start 111: 111 → 011 → 101 → 010 → 001 → 100 → 110 → wapas 111. Total 7 states (2^3 - 1 = 7), phir repeat. Yehi PRBS hai.
