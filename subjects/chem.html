Lubricants (properties & classification)
Lubricants: Functions & Classification· Dr. Anjali Saxena - Hindi
✎ Lubricants - quick exam notes (Instinct) - video nahi mila, khud padhao+

Lubricant: a substance between two moving surfaces that reduces friction and wear.

Functions: reduces friction and wear, carries away heat, seals out dust, prevents corrosion, and cushions shock between parts.

Classification: (1) Solid - graphite, molybdenum disulphide (used where oils would catch fire or leak); (2) Semi-solid - greases (oil + soap thickener; for slow, heavy loads); (3) Liquid - mineral oils (petroleum), vegetable and animal oils, blended oils (mineral oil + additives for viscosity index, oxidation stability).

Key properties: viscosity and viscosity index (how much viscosity changes with temperature), flash and fire point, cloud and pour point (low-temperature flow), oiliness (sticking to the surface), acid value, saponification value.

Exam tip: definitions + one example each of solid/semi-solid/liquid lubricant, and the meaning of viscosity index - this pattern repeats in papers.

MRI (application)
✎ MRI - ekdum simple (Instinct) - video nahi mila, khud padhao+

MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging) = body ke andar ki photo kheenchne ki machine, jo NMR spectroscopy ke principle pe kaam karti hai.

Jaise NMR me chhote molecules ka structure pata karte hain magnetic field daal ke, waise hi MRI me poori body (mostly paani ke hydrogen atoms) ka map banaya jata hai.
  • Body me hydrogen (paani) har jagah hai. Strong magnetic field + radio waves daalte hain.
  • Har tissue ke hydrogen alag signal dete hain - computer usse image banata hai.
  • Advantage: koi harmful radiation (X-ray) nahi, soft tissue ki saaf image.

Exam tip: "MRI is an application of NMR" - ye ek line likhna mat bhoolna.
