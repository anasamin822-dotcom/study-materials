CHEAT SHEET

Last-night revision

Key formulas and facts in one list - scan it the night before the paper.

  • Value education: understanding what is valuable (values) vs what is of use (skills) - both needed, values guide skills.
  • Basic human aspiration: continuous happiness (sukh) and prosperity (samriddhi).
  • Happiness = the state of being in harmony, within and with others; not momentary pleasure.
  • Prosperity = having more than enough physical facilities, and knowing it.
  • Right understanding + right feeling + physical facilities = human consciousness; animals live by assumption only.
  • The Self ('I') and the Body: Self has needs like trust/respect (continuous), Body needs food/shelter (temporary).
  • Sanyam (self-regulation) → Swasthya (health): the Self takes responsibility for the Body.
  • Harmony in family: justice - relationship based on trust (foundation) and respect (right evaluation).
  • Trust (vishwas): the assurance that the other intends my good. Respect = evaluating the other as they are.
  • Harmony in society: universal human order - undivided society (akhand samaj) with five dimensions: education, health, production, justice, exchange.
  • Nature's four orders: physical (soil/metal), bio (plants), animal, human - the first three are already in harmony; humans must understand it.
  • Existence = coexistence: units submerged in space; energy in equilibrium.
  • Professional ethics: using competence with humane values - competence guided by right understanding.
  • Svatantrata (self-organised) and Swarajya (self-rule): living by one's own natural acceptance, not external pressure.
