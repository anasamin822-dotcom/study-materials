Centre of gravity by integration
✎ Centre of Gravity by Integration - made simple (Instinct) - no video found, taught here+

Centre of gravity (CG) = the point where the whole weight feels balanced. Like the middle point of a balance scale.

Formulas (for a lamina/plate):

  • x̄ = (1/A) ∫∫ x dA   ,   ȳ = (1/A) ∫∫ y dA
  • where A = total area = ∫∫ dA

Steps: 1) Find the area A with a double integral. 2) Separately find ∫∫ x dA and ∫∫ y dA. 3) Divide each by A - done, you have (x̄, ȳ).

Think of tiny pizza slices. Add up each slice's "x contribution" (x dA), divide by the total area - you get the average x-position. That is the CG's x.

Exam tip: if the shape is symmetric (like a quadrant of a circle or a rectangle), the CG lies on the line of symmetry - half the work done.

Centre of gravity (CG) = wo point jahan poora weight balanced lagta hai. Jaise scale (tarazu) ka beech ka point.

Formula (lamina/plate ke liye):

  • x̄ = (1/A) ∫∫ x dA   ,   ȳ = (1/A) ∫∫ y dA
  • jahan A = total area = ∫∫ dA

Steps: 1) Pehle double integral se area A nikalo. 2) Phir ∫∫ x dA aur ∫∫ y dA alag-alag nikalo. 3) Dono ko A se divide karo - bas, (x̄, ȳ) mil gaya.

Socho pizza ke chhote-chhote tukde. Har tukde ka "x ka contribution" jodo (x dA), total area se divide karo - average x-position aa gayi. Wahi CG ka x hai.

Exam tip: Symmetric shape ho (jaise circle ka quadrant ya rectangle) to CG symmetry line pe hi hoga - kaam aadha.

CHEAT SHEET

Last-night revision

Key formulas and facts in one list - scan it the night before the paper.

  • d/dx x^n = n·x^(n−1); d/dx e^x = e^x; d/dx ln x = 1/x.
  • d/dx sin x = cos x; d/dx cos x = −sin x; d/dx tan x = sec²x.
  • Product rule: (uv)' = u'v + uv'. Quotient rule: (u/v)' = (u'v − uv')/v². Chain rule: dy/dx = dy/du · du/dx.
  • ∫x^n dx = x^(n+1)/(n+1) + C (n ≠ −1); ∫(1/x)dx = ln|x| + C; ∫e^x dx = e^x + C.
  • ∫sin x dx = −cos x + C; ∫cos x dx = sin x + C; ∫sec²x dx = tan x + C.
  • Integration by parts: ∫u dv = uv − ∫v du - pick u by ILATE (Inverse, Log, Algebraic, Trig, Exponential).
  • Maxima/minima: f'(x) = 0 gives critical points; f''(x) < 0 → max, f''(x) > 0 → min.
  • Partial derivatives: differentiate treating other variables as constants.
  • Gradient ∇f = (fx, fy, fz) - direction of steepest increase; divergence = ∇·F, curl = ∇×F.
  • Dot product a·b = |a||b|cosθ; cross product |a×b| = |a||b|sinθ, perpendicular to both.
  • 2×2 inverse: for [[a,b],[c,d]], inverse = (1/det)[[d,−b],[−c,a]], det = ad − bc (must be non-zero).
  • Rank of a matrix = number of non-zero rows in echelon form.
  • System Ax = b: unique solution if rank(A) = rank([A|b]) = n; infinite if ranks equal but < n; none if unequal.
  • Eigenvalues: solve det(A − λI) = 0; eigenvectors from (A − λI)x = 0.
